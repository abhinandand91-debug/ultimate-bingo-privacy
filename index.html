Who this is for

The game is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you think a child under 13 has used the game, write to us and we will delete the information.

What stays on your device

You do not need an account. Your progress, your unlocked levels, your power-ups, your high score and your settings (such as sound on or off) are saved on your device only. If you uninstall the game or clear its storage in your Android settings, this is deleted.

What we collect

  • Optional play statistics. The first time you open the game it asks whether you are happy to share anonymous play statistics (for example which level you played, your score and which power-ups you used). It does not include your name, email, location or any identifier that tells us who you are. In this version of the game these statistics are kept on your device and are not sent to us or anyone else. If a later version sends them anywhere, we will update this policy first and ask you again. You can say no and play exactly the same.
  • Purchases. The game offers optional in-app purchases of power-up packs. Purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive confirmation from Google Play that a purchase happened, not your payment details, and we use it only to give you the items you bought.
  • The Unity engine. The game is made with the Unity engine. Unity's software in the game may process technical information such as your device type, operating system version and app version, as described in Unity's privacy policy.

What we do not collect

Your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, advertising ID, or any payment card details. The game has no advertising. We do not run our own servers for this game and do not receive your game data.

Permissions the game uses

  • Internet and network state: so Google Play can process purchases.
  • Google Play Billing: to let you buy power-up packs.

How we use information

To run the game, remember your progress, and process the purchases you choose to make.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Purchases are processed by Google Play, which has its own privacy policy.

Your choices and rights

You can decline the play statistics question, delete the game's data by uninstalling it or clearing its storage, and ask Google Play about your purchase history in your Google account. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California) you may have the right to see, correct or delete your data and to object to some uses. Write to abhinandand91@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
